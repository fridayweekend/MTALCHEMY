MT ALCHEMY | EMOTIONAL CLARITY WORKBOOK

This reflection resource is for learning and personal practice. It is not a clinical assessment or a substitute for professional support.

1. ARRIVE
Pause for one minute. Notice your breathing, posture and energy without judging what you notice.

2. NAME THE MOMENT
What happened? Write only what a camera could have recorded. Separate facts from assumptions.

3. NOTICE YOUR RESPONSE
What emotion are you noticing? Where do you feel it in your body? What might the emotion be asking you to pay attention to?

4. IDENTIFY WHAT MATTERS
What need, value or boundary matters in this situation? What would a thoughtful response protect?

5. CHOOSE A NEXT STEP
Write one response you could take. Make it specific, respectful and small enough to do within the next day.

6. REFLECT
Afterward, what did you learn about yourself? What would you repeat or adjust next time?
