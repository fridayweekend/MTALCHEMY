MT ALCHEMY | FACILITATOR REFLECTION GUIDE

Use this short guide to prepare and debrief a reflective team-learning session. Adapt it to the group's context and provide participants with the option to pass on any activity.

BEFORE THE SESSION
- Agree the learning objective and the time available.
- Set expectations for confidentiality, respect and voluntary participation.
- Prepare a quiet check-in and a practical closing activity.

OPENING CHECK-IN
Invite each participant to share one word for how they are arriving. Listening is enough; avoid asking people to explain more than they wish.

DISCUSSION PROMPTS
- What helped the group listen to one another?
- When did a different perspective change the conversation?
- What made it easier or harder to contribute?
- What is one behaviour the team wants to practise?

DEBRIEF
1. Ask participants what they noticed.
2. Connect observations to the stated learning objective.
3. Invite one practical action per person or team.
4. Confirm how the group will revisit the action.
